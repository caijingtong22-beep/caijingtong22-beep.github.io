About me关于我

I graduated from Imperial College London with an MRes in Medical Robotics and Image Guided Intervention, with a background in Computer Science from Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and the University of Liverpool.

My work spans multimodal learning, human-computer interaction and physiological signal analysis — from learning with images, language and music to studying human experience in VR and simulated surgery.

Master’s thesis硕士毕业论文

Mapping Cortical Haemodynamic Responses to Cognitive Workload During Simulated Laparoscopic Surgery Using fNIRS基于 fNIRS 的模拟腹腔镜手术认知负荷与皮层血流动力学响应分析

Supervised by 导师：Prof. Daniel R. Leff, with Prof. Felipe Orihuela-Espina and Virginia C. H. Caddick.；共同指导：Prof. Felipe Orihuela-Espina、Virginia C. H. Caddick。

Analysed fNIRS recordings from 30 surgeons performing simulated laparoscopic surgery. Modelled HbO₂/HbR responses with event-related GLMs to examine intrinsic and extraneous cognitive workload across task phases.分析 30 名外科医生在模拟腹腔镜手术中的 fNIRS 数据，通过事件相关一般线性模型（GLM）建模 HbO₂/HbR 响应，研究不同手术阶段的内在与外在认知负荷。

Methods & findings方法与研究结果

Aligned 61-channel physiological signals with task and high-load event annotations. Used planned contrasts, ROI analyses, mixed models and robust sensitivity analyses. No primary result survived BH-FDR correction; observed regional and phase-dependent patterns remain exploratory.

将 61 通道生理信号与手术阶段、高负荷事件对齐，结合预设对比、ROI 分析、混合效应模型与稳健性分析。主要分析结果均未通过 BH-FDR 多重比较校正，观察到的区域性与阶段性模式属于探索性发现。

Undergraduate thesis本科毕业论文

Multimodal Music Emotional Recognition Based on Lyrics and Audio基于歌词与音频的多模态音乐情感识别

Supervised by 导师：Dr. Jacopo de Berardinis

Combined audio descriptors with lyrical text features to predict musical emotion on the Deezer Mood Detection Dataset. Compared early and late fusion; MLP-based late fusion achieved the highest R² among the evaluated configurations.基于 Deezer Mood Detection Dataset，融合音频描述符与歌词文本特征进行音乐情感预测。比较早期融合与后期融合策略，基于 MLP 的后期融合在所评估配置中取得最高 R²。

Methods & models方法与模型

Reproduced a multimodal baseline, then explored decision-level fusion of modality-specific models. Evaluated MLR, SVR, random forest and MLP regressors with audio features, TF-IDF and sentiment scores for valence and arousal prediction.

复现多模态基线，并探索各模态独立建模后的决策级融合。结合音频特征、TF-IDF 和情感分数，评估 MLR、SVR、随机森林与 MLP 在效价和唤醒度预测中的表现。
